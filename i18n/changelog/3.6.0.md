# 3.6.0

- "Maximale Qualität" is available in the start dialog: the full large-v3 model, decoded in windows of at most 29 seconds cut at speech pauses. It writes whole sentences, keeps timecodes closer to the picture and no longer falls into repetition loops. On an RTX 5080 a 51-minute interview takes about three minutes (turbo: about two). The model (3.1 GB) downloads once, on first use.
- The app now ships this fork's own engine build (server v0.6.10-sptv.1). Its fix: voice activity detection merged all continuous speech into a single window, so "stable timestamps" had no effect on interviews.
